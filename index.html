    FAQ

    How long does a website take?

    Simple one-page sites can take as little as 48 hours. Larger builds with more pages, menu items, or features take longer. Timelines also depend on how quickly I receive your content. I'll give you a real estimate once I know your scope.

    Do I need hosting?

    Yes, a site needs to live somewhere. Hosting fees aren't included, but I'll set it up with you and help with deployment.

    Do I need a domain?

    A domain (like yourrestaurant.com) is the address people type. Registration isn't included, and I'll walk you through getting one and connecting it.

    Can I update my menu?

    You can edit it yourself if you're comfortable with code, or add Monthly Updates and I'll make every change for you.

    Can you add online ordering?

    Not in the core packages, but I can scope and quote integrating a supported ordering platform where it fits.

    Can you add reservations?

    I can add reservation buttons that link to your booking system or contact flow, and scope supported integrations separately.

    Can you connect Instagram?

    Yes. Linking your profile and embedding feeds is typically part of the Full Package.

    How do payments work?

    50% upfront to begin work and 50% on completion, before final files or deployment. See Before We Start below.

    How many revisions do I get?

    1 round with Basic, 2 with Website + Menu, 3 with the Full Package. Extra rounds can be arranged.

    Do you maintain websites?

    Ongoing maintenance isn't automatic, but Monthly Updates cover menu and content changes if you want continued support.

    What do you need from me?

    See the list in Before we start, I'll need.
